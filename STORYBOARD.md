# Exhibition loop / v2

10 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, silent, continuous landscape composition.

0.00–1.80 — SCAN. One oversized invoice, actual before/after reveal with a scanning beam. Brand and URL are already visible at frame zero.
1.80–4.30 — CLEAN. The same invoice shifts sideways as actual passport and ID outputs fan out from it. This is an editorial product montage, not a recording of a multi-document action.
4.15–7.35 — KEEP IT YOURS. Papers converge as the real review screen arrives; an editorial wipe reveals the real export screen. Privacy copy is sized to read from across a room.
7.05–9.60 — Official ribbon symbol and large wordmark arrive on the same stage. Upload nothing. Persistent URL remains visible throughout.
9.25–10.00 — Paper and opening title return. Every animated element has matching states at exactly 0 and 10 seconds.

Distance hierarchy: headline 180–226 px; main product copy 46–49 px; URL 40 px; masthead 48 px. Small peripheral labels are supporting texture, not required reading. Brand, URL, headline and main proof remain in action/title safe areas.

BANNED: split light slides, paragraph copy, sequential full-page screenshot slides, fake cursor interactions, camera creep, idle bouncing, UI tilted in 3D. Paper outputs rotate in 2D as editorial objects.
